YOUTUBE TITLE

Give ChatGPT a Job, Not Just a Prompt | Agent Academy Ep. 2

YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION

A vague request leaves ChatGPT with a lot to figure out. A clear job gives it a result to work toward, boundaries to follow, and a place to stop.

In Episode 2 of Agent Academy, I walk through a practical browser demo: turning fictional client notes into a project brief that a human can review.

We start with a broad request, then give ChatGPT a more specific assignment using a five-part job brief:

• Result: What should it produce?
• Audience: Who needs the result?
• Allowed information and tools: What may it use?
• Checks: What makes the result useful?
• Stopping point: Where does the job end?

Along the way, we look at clarifying questions, unresolved decisions, and when a draft needs a precise correction. The second conversation also receives additional answers from me, so we examine how those answers help shape the result.

One important distinction: writing a boundary into a prompt does not configure an app’s actual access permissions.

Your assignment: choose one small task, define the job, run it once, and check the result before taking the next step.
